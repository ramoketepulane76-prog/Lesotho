Activities in Lesotho

Enjoy the outdoors, discover Basotho heritage and experience the beauty of the highlands at your own pace.

Hiking and nature walks

Explore mountain trails and scenic viewpoints. Choose routes suitable for your fitness and experience, and check weather conditions before setting out.

Horse Riding

Discover the highlands on a guided horse riding. Follow the guide's instructions and use appropriate protective equipment.

Culture and heritage

Learn about Basotho traditions, traditional clothing, crafts, music, food and the history of important heritage sites.

Photography and sightseeing

Capture mountain sunrises, valleys, dams and village life respectfully. Ask permission before photographing people.

Frequently asked questions

Pack layered clothing, comfortable walking shoes, water, snacks, sun protection and a charged phone. Bring warmer clothing when conditions may be cold.

Ask a reputable local operator about beginner-friendly routes, safety instructions, equipment and age or health restrictions before booking.

Dress appropriately for the setting, ask before taking photographs, follow local guidance and treat cultural sites with care.
